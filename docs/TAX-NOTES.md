# Tax notes

This is a formatting tool, not tax advice. Rules change; confirm with your employer's payroll team or a chartered accountant.

| Topic | What the tool does |
|---|---|
| Receipt wording | Follows the common Section 10(13A) format employers accept: amount in figures and words, payer, payment mode and date, period, property, owner name, address and PAN, signature line. |
| Landlord PAN | Employers usually need it when yearly rent is above INR 1,00,000. The form warns if it is missing, and rejects a PAN that is not `AAAAA9999A`. |
| Revenue stamp | Cash rent above INR 5,000 needs a Re.1 stamp. The stamp line is on by default. |
| TDS on rent | A tenant who is an individual pays 5% TDS under Section 194-IB once rent reaches INR 50,000 a month. The tool shows a note. The receipt is unchanged. |
| Future dates | Each receipt is dated with its payment date. The tool warns about receipts dated in the future, because a receipt should record rent that was actually paid. |
| Escalation | Rent rises by the percentage you enter, once per interval, from the "rent in force since" date. Any month can be overridden by hand. |
| Partial months | Prorated by days by default, or charged in full if you switch the setting. |
